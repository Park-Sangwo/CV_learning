## 1. Pinhole Camera and Camera Obscura (1558)

Concept dates back to 500 B.C. (Chinese philosophers) and 1000 A.D. (Arabian philosophers and scientists).

Popularized in the West during the 16th century among artists.

Mechanism - tiny pinhole in the wall projects a 3D scene onto the opposite wall to create a 2D image.

Pros & Cons - powerful in terms of sharpness, but image is very dim because only a tiny amount of light passes through.

Lens-Based Camera Obscura (1568) - enhanced with a lens to create brighter images for artists' convenience.


## 2. Invention of Film (1837) and Color Film (1877)

Invention of film - considered a game-changer; allowed recording a moment by simply pressing a button without an artist.

Daguerreotype camera (1837) - capture the first permanent photographs (ex - Still Life)

Chemical mechanism - utilizes a layer of silver halide; when exposed to light, it converts to metallic silver depending on brightness.

Color photography (1877/1887) - using a silver halide layer with an additional dye coupler containing color pigments.

Consumer film cameras (1920s/1928) - memorialize anything


## 3. Silicon Image Detector (1970) and Digital Cameras (1994)

Silicon Image Detector (1970) - can do exactly what film does, but does not need to be replaced after each capture (infinite image capture).

Explosion of digital cameras in the marketplace occurred in the early 1990 after the sensor matured.

Nikon COOLPIX - perhaps the most popular early consumer digital camera.

(Early camera characteristics : captured a 640x480 image, very power-hungry requiring frequent battery replacement or recharge)


## 4. Phones with Cameras (2007)

Smartphones with cameras entered the market around the end of the 20th century.

Technical milestone - required extreme miniaturization of the camera while simultaneously improving performance.

Apple iPhone 1 (2007) - included a tiny camera that gave rise to daily visual communication via social platforms (Snapchat, Instagram).

Served as a core catalyst for the major advances in computer vision and artificial intelligence witnessed in the last decade.
